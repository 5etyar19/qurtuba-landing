About Qurtuba

Who we are and what we do in Jordan.

Qurtuba For Trading Foodstuffs Est. is a Jordanian food trading and distribution company based in Amman, the capital of Jordan. We specialise in sourcing, importing and wholesale supply of food and beverage products for the Jordanian market.

We buy directly from producers and exporters abroad, in tonnage, ship the goods to Jordan on our own account, and sell them to businesses across the Kingdom. We focus on reliable supply, strong relationships and professional service.

What we do

We deliver to retailers, wholesalers and food businesses across Jordan.
